Import from Garmin Connect

  1. Record the session on your watch as a Strength or Cardio activity.
  2. In Garmin Connect on the web, open the activity, click the gear icon and choose Export to TCX (or Export Original for a .fit file, unzipped).
  3. Drop the file here. It is matched to the SWEAT workout you did at the same time.

Export your whole activity list as CSV from Garmin Connect's Activities page to import many at once.

Live connection

Automatic sync needs approval from Garmin's Connect Developer Program, which only approved businesses get. Until then, import files here. In Claude, the athletedata connector can read your Garmin data and send workouts to your watch.
